# Documentary Website and App

Turn a prompt or topic into a documentary video.

## What it does

Give a prompt or topic, choose a language — Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hindi, or English — and choose a length from 1 minute to 1 hour. The app writes the scenes, generates a matching picture for every scene, plays the video with best-voice narration and matching subtitles, and offers a .webm video download and .srt subtitles. Long videos build in batches.

## Features

- Scene-matched cinematic pictures — a picture for every scene
- Best voice picker and speed control for narration
- Matching subtitles
- .webm video download and .srt subtitles download
- YouTube Publish Kit — title, description, and hashtags
- Long videos build in batches
